Side shows shares held (U/D); Entry is the pair cost once both legs exist. A leg that never gets hedged settles as a plain directional bet — at most one leg is unhedged at a time. Budget per pair covers both legs: $10 buys 10 shares of the first side. Orders fill like real ones: after the order delay, against the live book, only at or under the price seen, minimum 5 shares. Settles on Polymarket's own resolution, ~6-10 min after close.
